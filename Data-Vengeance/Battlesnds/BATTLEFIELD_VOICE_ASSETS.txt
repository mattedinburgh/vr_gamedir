Design
------
The shared CC0 bank is an occasional alternative to Vengeance's native
per-class/per-voice taunts, not a replacement. The release data already contains
5,742 voice-taunt OGG files; 4,803 are in combat-event categories. This gives the
event router a very large reaction pool without duplicating or padding the mod
with irrelevant announcer clips.

A 1.8 second sector-wide gate prevents the shared military callouts from
overlapping into noise. Existing per-soldier taunt delays remain active.

Semantic callout seed aliases (2026-09-17)
-------------------------------------------
The UI / Tactical Information / Audio stream also reuses 14 of the CC0 files
above under exact subtitle-matched semantic names (7 phrases x male/female).
These aliases are used only where the recorded phrase exactly matches the
selected English popup line; emotion-specific rewritten subtitles never fall
back to a mismatched recording.

Seeded semantic phrases: Fire in the hole!, Get down!, Hold!, Medic!,
Reloading!, RPG!, Suppressing fire!.
The complete 510-row production/recording contract is generated and validated
in vr_source under Tools/Audio/BATTLE_CALLOUT_RECORDING_MANIFEST.csv.
