Vengeance map ambience expansion
================================

These WAV files are original procedural project assets authored for the Vengeance ambience layer.
They contain no sampled speech, music, or third-party recordings.

VR_CITY_DAY.wav         - low, intermittent distant traffic bed
VR_CITY_NIGHT.wav       - quieter night traffic bed
VR_CITY_HORN.wav        - distant dual-tone vehicle horn
VR_CITY_JINGLE.wav      - short neutral four-note street/shop chime
VR_FARM_BELL.wav        - sparse farm/cow-bell style metallic cue
VR_INDUSTRIAL_CLANK.wav - sparse industrial metal impact

Existing Vengeance assets BIRD4/6/7/9.wav, SOUNDS/COWMOO3.wav and SOUNDS/HELI1.WAV
are also reused by the source-side sector ambience mapper.

Format: PCM WAV, mono, 22050 Hz, 16-bit.
Design rule: ambience is intentionally sparse so combat readability is preserved.
